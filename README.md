# athanor-skew-braces

Skew braces and the Yang–Baxter equation (L. Guarnieri, L. Vendramin, Math. Comp. 86 (2017) 2519–2534, arXiv:1511.03171):
independent checks, open problems and status.

Athanor lab (Aleksei Kudriashov, ORCID 0009-0006-8885-5338). Chosen by the lab's radar on 15 November 2026 (rank 4: OpenAlex
citations and recent citation rate, papers with checkable open problems). Work by independent agents, each result with a
certificate (two independent programs or a complete written argument), a prior-art record and the guide's own check before it
is called a result. ChatGPT research agents (OpenAI) compute and write; Anthropic Claude, the lab's guide, orchestrates and
verifies; the author of record takes responsibility.
